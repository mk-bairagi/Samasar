## Full description (4000 char max)

**English**

```
Samasar brings you news from where you actually live.

Most news apps show you the country. Samasar starts with your district — all 50
in Madhya Pradesh, from Indore and Bhopal to Neemuch, Dindori and Sheopur — and
then widens out to the state and the nation. Three taps, three scopes, in that
order.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

• YOUR DISTRICT FIRST
  Choose your district once. Local reporting leads every time you open the app.

• SEE HOW MANY SOURCES AGREE
  When several publishers carry the same story, Samasar groups them into one and
  shows you the count. A story ten papers report is different from one nobody
  else has — and you can see which is which, and read whichever account you
  prefer.

• HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT DOES NOT KNOW
  Single-source stories are labelled as such. When a quiet district has no local
  news, the app says so instead of quietly passing off state coverage as local.

• BUILT FOR HINDI
  Headlines in Hindi from Amar Ujala, Dainik Bhaskar, Patrika and more, with
  district names as people actually say them.

• A FEED YOU CONTROL
  Swipe away stories you do not want. Mute a publisher. Mute a keyword — a name,
  a tournament, a subject you are tired of. All of it reversible, all of it on
  your phone.

• READS WELL OFFLINE
  Recent headlines are cached, so a weak signal shows yesterday's news rather
  than an error.

• NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
  No sign-up. No advertising. No analytics. Nothing about your reading leaves
  your device.

HOW IT WORKS

Samasar gathers headlines from over 120 Indian news sources, groups stories that
report the same event, and links you to the publisher to read in full. Every
story credits its source, and tapping it takes you to their site. Samasar does
not reproduce full articles.

Coverage: all 50 Madhya Pradesh districts in Hindi, plus national news in Hindi,
English and Gujarati.
```

## Content rating questionnaire

Answer honestly; a news app typically lands at **Teen / PEGI 12** because it
links to real-world reporting that can include violence or crime.

- Violence: **No** — the app contains none itself, it links to news
- Sexuality, profanity, drugs, gambling: **No**
- User-generated content: **No** — readers cannot post anything
- Shares location: **No**
- Digital purchases: **No**

## Data safety declaration

- **Does your app collect or share any user data?** → **No**
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** → Yes (HTTPS)
- **Can users request data deletion?** → Not applicable; nothing is collected.
  Uninstalling removes everything stored locally.

This is unusually simple because it is true: there is no account system, no
analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the build.
