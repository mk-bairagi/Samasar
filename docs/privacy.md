# Privacy Policy — Samasar

**Last updated:** 15 August 2026

Samasar is a news reader for Madhya Pradesh. This policy explains, plainly, what
the app does and does not do with information.

## Short version

**Samasar has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.** It does
not collect personal information, and there is no server storing anything about
you. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

## What Samasar stores

All of the following is kept **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your chosen district | So the app opens on your local news |
| Saved stories | So your reading list survives offline |
| Hidden stories | So a story you dismissed stays dismissed |
| Muted publishers and keywords | So your filters keep working |
| Appearance and layout settings | Dark mode, compact feed |
| A cache of recently downloaded headlines | So the app works on a poor connection |

Uninstalling Samasar, or clearing its data in Android Settings, removes all of it
permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

## What Samasar does not collect

- No name, email address, phone number or account
- No contacts, photos, files or microphone access
- No location. The app never requests location permission — you choose your
  district from a list instead
- No advertising identifier, and no advertising
- No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind
- No profile of what you read

The app requests exactly two Android permissions: **internet access** and
**network state**, both required to download headlines.

## Information that reaches other companies

Samasar has no servers of its own, but using any internet app means some
information necessarily reaches the services it connects to. Being specific:

**GitHub** hosts the headline file the app downloads. When Samasar refreshes,
GitHub can see your device's IP address, as any web server would. GitHub's
handling of that is covered by the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**News publishers** host the article images shown in the feed. When an image
loads, that publisher's server sees your IP address.

**When you tap a story**, Samasar opens the publisher's own website in your
browser. From that point you are on their site and **their** privacy policy
applies, not this one. Samasar does not reproduce full articles.

None of these receive any identifier from Samasar — no account, no device ID, no
advertising ID. They see only what any website sees when a browser connects.

## Children

Samasar is a general news application and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## News content

Samasar aggregates publicly available headlines from Indian news publishers and
links back to the original source. All articles remain the property of their
publishers, who are credited on every story. Samasar does not store or reproduce
full article text.

If you are a publisher and would like your content removed, contact the address
below and it will be removed promptly.
